Wildfire Preparedness

  • Defensible space. Maintain at least 30 ft of cleared, well-watered vegetation around structures; 100 ft on slopes.
  • Burn restrictions. Any currently-in-effect orders for the basin are listed at the top of this page. Check Utah Fire Info for the full statewide picture. Restrictions tighten on Red Flag days.
  • Evacuation plan. Know two escape routes from your area, and a rendezvous point off the canyon.
  • Go-bag. 72 hours of water, meds, documents, N95s, and pet supplies near the door during Red Flag periods.
  • Report new fires. Call 911 immediately. Note the location, smoke color, and any visible flames.
