About BasinWx

What this site is

BasinWx shows live weather and air-quality observations, and experimental forecasts, for the Uintah Basin in northeastern Utah. It is built and run by the Bingham Research Center at Utah State University.

Everything here is a research product. Forecasts are not official and live data are preliminary. For official forecasts and warnings, use the National Weather Service and the Utah Division of Air Quality.

Winter ozone

In some winters, ozone near the ground in the Basin rises above the federal health standard. It builds when snow cover, a temperature inversion, sunshine and light winds come together, holding emissions in a shallow, sunlit layer of cold air.

The Ozone Alert outlooks are written by our forecasters during the winter season.

Clyfar

Clyfar is our in-house model for winter ozone. During the ozone season it runs on each new NOAA GEFS ensemble weather forecast, four times a day, and estimates how likely elevated ozone is up to two weeks or more ahead. It is experimental and has not been operationally validated. See the latest Clyfar forecast.

Where the data come from

  • Observations: weather and air-quality stations, through Synoptic Data. Some data come from partners including Utah DEQ and the Ute Indian Tribe.
  • Weather models: NOAA's HRRR and GEFS.
  • Roads: UDOT.
  • Fire: NASA FIRMS satellite detections, Utah Fire Info restrictions, National Weather Service and Open-Meteo forecasts.
